Zero-Copy Kernel

Deforming 16,384 triangles, smooth normals, and strain energy inside linear memory.

crates/wasm-sim compiles directly to wasm32-unknown-unknown as a zero-dependency cdylib. A cyclic 128 × 64 parametric manifold morphs smoothly across four 3D forms while updating contiguous positions, normals, and per-vertex energies buffers read straight into Three.js GLSL.

Axum & Askama
